# Six-university source check — 17 September 2026

Original frozen research remains unchanged. This supplement replaces generic research placeholders with sourced criteria. Recurring dates are displayed without inventing target-cycle timestamps. Academic-year estimates are not next-intake quotes. School inputs are self-reported and do not imply an admissions offer.

## University of Wisconsin–Madison

- **Academic preparation**: Minimum school coursework: English 4 years, math 3, social science 3, natural science 3 and electives 4. Math must include algebra, geometry and advanced math; world language is recommended, not a separate admission gate. [Official source](https://admissions.wisc.edu/first-year-academic-requirements/)
- **English routes**: IELTS Academic: overall 6.5. [Official source](https://admissions.wisc.edu/international/)
- **SAT / ACT policy**: SAT/ACT is optional for Fall 2027 first-year applicants. [Official source](https://admissions.wisc.edu/apply-as-a-freshman/)
- **Application documents**: Secondary academic records are required. The applicant must confirm the curriculum-specific checklist, translations and official delivery. [Official source](https://admissions.wisc.edu/international/)
- **Application dates**: Regular application 2027-01-15; supporting materials 2027-01-22. America/Los_Angeles. [Official source](https://admissions.wisc.edu/deadlines/)
- **Published cost breakdown**: 2026–27 nonresident COA USD 65,120 plus first-year fee 330 and computer allowance 1,100 = 66,550. International fees and insurance are additional. Housing/food 14,994 is included. [Official source](https://financialaid.wisc.edu/cost-of-attendance/)

## University of Waterloo

- **Academic preparation**: IB: 32 subject points, excluding diploma bonus points; six courses, at least three HL. Math AA HL at least 6. English A HL/SL or English B HL at least 5. Diploma and AIF required. [Official source](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-students/admissions/admission-requirements/computer-science/high-school/international-system/ib)
- **English routes**: IELTS Academic: overall 6.5; reading 6, writing 6.5, listening 6, speaking 6.5. [Official source](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-students/admissions/english-language-requirements)
- **Application documents**: Applicants complete DAE, AIF and the CS Supplementary Information Form and submit required academic records. [Official source](https://uwaterloo.ca/computer-science/future-undergraduate-students/applying-admissions)
- **Application dates**: Regular application 2027-02-01; supporting materials 2027-02-15. Timezone not frozen. [Official source](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-students/admissions/application-deadlines)
- **Published cost breakdown**: 2026–27, two study terms (8 months): international CS tuition/fees CAD 73,000 + books 1,500 + Village 1 double / meal plan B 16,294 = 90,794. Housing is a selected example; co-op fees, personal costs and travel are additional. [Official source](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-students/financing/tuition)

## Georgia Institute of Technology

- **Academic preparation**: International coursework is reviewed in the context of the school system and available rigor. The USG unit list applies to US-system schooling; it is not automatically imposed on an overseas IB applicant. [Official source](https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year/academic-preparation)
- **English routes**: IELTS Academic minimum 6 overall. [Official source](https://www.usg.edu/international-education/esl-programs/english-proficiency-requirements/)
- **SAT / ACT policy**: First-year applicants must submit SAT or ACT scores. No guaranteed-admission score is stated. [Official source](https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year/standardized-tests)
- **Application documents**: Secondary academic records are required. The applicant must confirm the curriculum-specific checklist, translations and official delivery. [Official source](https://admission.gatech.edu/international/first-year)
- **Application dates**: Regular application 2027-01-06; supporting materials 2027-01-22. APPLICANT_LOCAL. [Official source](https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year/deadlines)
- **Published cost breakdown**: 2026–27 international first year, on campus: tuition/fees USD 37,326; housing/food 14,628; books 800; travel/personal 3,766. Published total 56,520; off campus 60,286. Check health insurance separately. [Official source](https://finaid.gatech.edu/costs/undergraduate-costs)

## Purdue University

- **Academic preparation**: English 4 years; math 4; laboratory science 3; social studies 3; world language 2. [Official source](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/course-requirements/)
- **English routes**: IELTS usually 6.5 overall / 6.0 each; DET usually 115 / 110 each. Tests must be within two years. These are published guidelines, not guaranteed admission cutoffs. [Official source](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/english-proficiency/)
- **SAT / ACT policy**: SAT, ACT or CLT considered if submitted; scores must be within five years. No minimum total is stated on the first-year criteria page. [Official source](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/first-year-criteria/)
- **Application documents**: School records, essay and Purdue questions; English evidence. Kazakhstan: years 8–9 and 10–11 records; graduates also provide the final certificate. Certified English translations. Non-English-speaking countries waive the world-language requirement. [Official source](https://admissions.purdue.edu/become-student/international/)
- **Application dates**: November 1: CS priority / Early Action. January 15: regular deadline, only if CS still has space. Published recurring dates; confirm the target cycle in the application portal. [Official source](https://admissions.purdue.edu/deadlines/first-year-college-student/)
- **Published cost breakdown**: 2026–27 international CS: tuition/fees USD 36,154 (includes USD 4,050 CS differential); housing/food 16,734; books 1,090; travel/personal 2,770. Published total 56,748. Insurance and international flights may add costs. [Official source](https://www.purdue.edu/treasurer/finance/bursar-office/tuition/fee-rates-2026-2027/undergraduate-tuition-and-fees-2026-2027/)

## Rochester Institute of Technology

- **Academic preparation**: CS: four years of math through precalculus; chemistry or physics required. General preparation includes four years of English and three of social studies. Diploma by enrolment; holistic selection. [Official source](https://www.rit.edu/admissions/first-year-application)
- **English routes**: Direct: IELTS 6.5 or DET 120. IELTS 6.0 or below may lead to conditional English study; academic requirements still apply. [Official source](https://www.rit.edu/admissions/international)
- **SAT / ACT policy**: SAT / ACT optional; the application is reviewed holistically with or without scores. [Official source](https://www.rit.edu/admissions/first-year-application)
- **Application documents**: Application, essay, school transcript, recommendation and English evidence; no art portfolio for Computer Science. [Official source](https://www.rit.edu/admissions/first-year-application)
- **Application dates**: Early Action / ED I: November 1; ED II: January 1; Regular Decision: January 15. Recurring published dates; ED is binding, Early Action is not. [Official source](https://www.rit.edu/admissions/first-year-application)
- **Published cost breakdown**: 2026–27: tuition USD 63,508; fees 1,100; housing/food 17,370; other 2,890. Published COA 84,868 plus first-year orientation 430 = 85,298. Health insurance is additional unless waived. [Official source](https://www.rit.edu/admissions/tuition-and-fees)

## Arizona State University

- **Academic preparation**: International baseline: GPA 3.00 / 4.00. Outside-US schooling: math 4 years and lab science 3. CS aptitude: SAT 1210, ACT 24, competency GPA 3.00 or top 25%; no math/science deficiencies. ASU evaluates equivalency. [Official source](https://degrees.asu.edu/bachelors/major/ASU00/ESCSEBS/computer-science)
- **English routes**: Computer Science: IELTS 6.5; DET 105; PTE 58. TOEFL: 79 on the old scale, or 4 overall with 3.5 each on tests from 21 January 2026. [Official source](https://degrees.asu.edu/bachelors/major/ASU00/ESCSEBS/computer-science)
- **SAT / ACT policy**: SAT / ACT can meet a CS aptitude alternative; they do not replace the international GPA or subject requirements. [Official source](https://degrees.asu.edu/bachelors/major/ASU00/ESCSEBS/computer-science)
- **Application documents**: Application and fee, three years of school records, certified English translations and official English scores. Final diploma required for enrolment. [Official source](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/international/first-year)
- **Application dates**: Fall 2027: November 1, 2026 priority; January 15, 2027 regular admission date. Apply early for scholarship consideration. [Official source](https://admission.asu.edu/apply/first-year/admission)
- **Published cost breakdown**: 2026–27 published estimate USD 69,906: tuition/fees 43,009; housing/food 18,819; books 1,320; travel/personal 3,993; insurance 2,765. ASU still uses the 2025–26 insurance amount and the highest college fee; your bill may differ. [Official source](https://admission.asu.edu/cost-aid/international)
